Goop Land

A brutal souls-like set in a frozen, clouded wasteland where alien forces and barbaric clans collide.

Project Status

Early Development

Goop Land is currently in its early development stage, with core systems and gameplay mechanics actively being built and refined. The project is focused on establishing a strong foundation for combat, movement, and environmental interaction, while experimenting with tone, pacing, and player immersion.

The overall vision is to create a souls-like experience that blends harsh, methodical combat with a mysterious and atmospheric world. Set in a frozen, cloud-covered landscape, players will face brutal encounters against barbarian factions while uncovering traces of an alien presence that has shaped the world in unknown ways.

Goop Land aims to deliver a sense of isolation, tension, and discovery—where every encounter matters, and every piece of the world hints at a deeper, hidden narrative waiting to be uncovered.
