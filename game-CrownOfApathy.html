Goop Land

A chaotic, slime-filled world where everything sticks, stretches, and reacts.

Return to HQ

Project Status

Prototype / Early Development

Goop Land is an experimental physics-based game focused on fluid interactions, reactive environments, and unpredictable gameplay. Players explore a strange ecosystem made entirely of living slime, where movement, combat, and puzzles are all influenced by dynamic goo mechanics.
