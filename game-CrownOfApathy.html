Gameplay Preview

Early footage from Goop Land

Goop Land is a souls-like experience centered around precision combat, environmental storytelling, and atmospheric exploration. Players take control of a mysterious, otherworldly warrior navigating a desolate snow-covered landscape filled with hostile barbarian factions and remnants of alien influence.

The game emphasizes weighty combat, strategic encounters, and uncovering fragments of a forgotten civilization hidden beneath the ice and clouds.
